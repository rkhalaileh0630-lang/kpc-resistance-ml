# Source records

Keep the blank `literature/sources_template.csv` reusable. Copy it to
`literature/sources.csv` for your local bibliography. The working log is ignored
by Git; back up your originals and working files in your team's chosen location.

Use stable source IDs. For each publication, retain its title, authors, year,
DOI or URL, access date, and exact page/table/figure location. Record any reuse
restrictions in `redistribution_notes`. Record transcription corrections in
`extraction_notes`, including the date and reason.

Do not overwrite an original download when correcting a working copy. Keep a
short change log so another team member can trace a correction to its source.
Leave unknown metadata blank and record why it is missing.

The current software does not verify bibliography entries, resolve DOIs, check
that source IDs exist in this log, or judge publication quality. Review those
items manually. Do not interpret a format-check success as source verification.

The metadata-only toy fixture uses the invented ID `toy-source`; it is not a
real publication and must not be cited as evidence.
